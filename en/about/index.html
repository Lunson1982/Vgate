Company Overview

Established in Hong Kong in 2005, VGate Company Limited was founded to serve the fashion, lifestyle, and accessory markets. The company has expanded its operations through multiple strategies including exclusive distribution rights, joint ventures, and in-house development.

Starting from a single store, VGate grew rapidly to a peak of over 30 stores across premier shopping malls in Hong Kong, mainland China, Taiwan, Macau, and Singapore — a testament to strong business growth and retail operational capability.

Philosophy and Vision

As a fast-growing fashion and lifestyle brand retailer in the China-Pacific region, we are committed to introducing and developing high-quality yet affordable fashion, apparel, footwear, bags, and lifestyle accessories for customers who value style and taste.

Our goal is to create a dedicated space where fashion enthusiasts can fully express their unique charm, and to deliver a one-of-a-kind shopping experience.
